Our Craft

Designed around the arc of a stride, not the shape of a shelf.

Most shoes are designed to look good standing still. SOLARC starts with the arc your foot actually makes mid-stride — the roll from heel to toe — and builds the sole, upper and lacing around that single curve. The result is a shoe that feels the same whether you're sprinting for a train or standing in line for coffee.

Founded in Lucknow and built for anywhere, SOLARC is a small team obsessed with one shape, done properly, four different ways.
